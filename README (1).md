## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergrad specializing in AI, and I like building things that actually work end to end — not just prototypes. Most of my time goes into three areas: **retrieval-augmented systems**, **backend infrastructure**, and **understanding how things break under load**. I've spent time in a defense research lab exploring quantum algorithms, and in industry building RAG assistants and validating production APIs — but what I enjoy most is the part after the tutorial ends, where you have to actually make a design decision and live with it.

> *"Depth over buzzwords. Implementation over claims. Understanding over memorization."*

**🧠 [DocuMind](https://github.com/whoankitchauhan)** — a modular RAG system for document intelligence: ingestion, chunking, FAISS-based semantic retrieval, and conversational memory, built to support agentic workflows and tool calling.
<br>`Python` `FastAPI` `FAISS`

**🔗 [LinkForge](https://github.com/whoankitchauhan)** — a distributed link-management platform with Redis caching, RabbitMQ-driven async analytics, and full observability via Prometheus and Grafana.
<br>`Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `RabbitMQ`

**⚡ [CacheCore](https://github.com/whoankitchauhan)** — a concurrent in-memory key-value store built from scratch, with O(1) average GET/SET, LRU eviction, and TTL expiry, benchmarked under concurrent load.
<br>`C++` `STL` `Concurrency`
